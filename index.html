Exam-faithful mocks

80 questions sampled to the exam's subject weighting, the official 3½-hour countdown, a computer-flight-plan appendix block, flagging and a question palette — and no feedback until you submit, exactly like exam day. Results are scored by section with a full review of every question.

A flight plan generator, not one stale PDF

Every session builds a brand-new computer flight plan — fuel ladder, waypoint table, trip wind, shear point, altitude trade-offs — and asks questions whose answers come from that document. Infinite practice, never memorized answers.

Smart review that doesn't forget

Every answer is tracked. Missed questions return on a spaced schedule until they stick, and the dashboard estimates your readiness against the 70% pass mark — by section and by topic, so you always know what to study next.

Worked calculations & must-know numbers

Generated critical point, PNR, minimum fuel, ISA deviation and range problems with full worked solutions, plus a Library card of the memorization core for every topic — GFA issue times, icing bands, CAT rules of thumb, frequencies and more, each with its source reference.
